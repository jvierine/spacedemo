Equations

\(p_e=a_e(1-e_e^2)\)
\(v_{R,e}=\sqrt{\dfrac{\mu_E}{p_e}}e_e\sin\nu\)
\(v_{T,e}=\sqrt{\dfrac{\mu_E}{p_e}}(1+e_e\cos\nu)\)

\(a_{GTO}=\dfrac{r_b+r_{GEO}}{2},\qquad v_{GTO,p}=\sqrt{\mu_E\left(\dfrac{2}{r_b}-\dfrac{1}{a_{GTO}}\right)}\)

\(\Delta v_R=-v_{R,e},\qquad \Delta v_T=v_{GTO,p}-v_{T,e}\)
\(\Delta\mathbf v_1=\Delta v_R\hat{\mathbf r}+\Delta v_T\hat{\boldsymbol\theta},\qquad \Delta v_1=\sqrt{\Delta v_R^2+\Delta v_T^2}\)

\(\Delta v_2=\sqrt{\dfrac{\mu_E}{r_{GEO}}}-v_{GTO,a}\)

The entry-orbit velocity is calculated from its Keplerian elements in the local radial–transverse frame. Burn 1 cancels its radial component, sets the required tangential GTO periapsis speed, and is then rotated into the plotted Cartesian frame. Burn 2 raises perigee and circularizes. Inclination change and losses are omitted.
