Equations

\(v=\sqrt{\mu_E\left(\dfrac{2}{r}-\dfrac{1}{a}\right)}\)

\(a_{GTO}=\dfrac{r_p+r_{GEO}}{2},\qquad \Delta v_1=v_{GTO,p}-v_{park,p}\)

\(\Delta v_2=\sqrt{\dfrac{\mu_E}{r_{GEO}}}-v_{GTO,a}\)

The off-perigee burn is tangential and is chosen to match standard GTO energy. Except for a circular parking orbit, matching energy alone does not match both GTO apsides.
