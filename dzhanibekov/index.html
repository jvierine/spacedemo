Dzhanibekov effect

Torque-free rotation is stable around the smallest and largest principal moments, but unstable around the intermediate moment. A tiny perturbation grows into repeated 180° flips.

\(I_1\dot\omega_1=(I_2-I_3)\omega_2\omega_3\)
\(I_2\dot\omega_2=(I_3-I_1)\omega_3\omega_1\)
\(I_3\dot\omega_3=(I_1-I_2)\omega_1\omega_2\)

Principal moments

All axes use the same discrete scale. The selected moment moves through its full range; the other moments adjust by the smallest amount needed to retain a physical cuboid.
