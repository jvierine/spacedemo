Atmospheric drag

Drag removes orbital energy. It acts most strongly near perigee, lowering apogee and circularizing an eccentric orbit before re-entry.

\(\dfrac{da}{dt}\approx-\rho C_D\dfrac{A}{m}\sqrt{\mu a}\)
