Fortescue equation 4.38 gives the accumulated first-order apsidal angle:

\[\bar\omega(t)=\omega_0+\frac{3}{2}J_2n\left(\frac{R_E}{p}\right)^2\left(2-\frac{5}{2}\sin^2i\right)t+O(J_2^2)\]

Differentiating gives its rate. The apparent difference is only this trigonometric rearrangement:

\[\frac{3}{2}\left(2-\frac{5}{2}\sin^2i\right)=\frac{3}{4}(5\cos^2i-1)\]\[\dot{\bar\omega}=\frac{3}{4}J_2n\left(\frac{R_E}{p}\right)^2(5\cos^2i-1)\]\[\dot\Omega=-\frac{3}{2}J_2n\left(\frac{R_E}{p}\right)^2\cos i\]
