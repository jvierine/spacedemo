Variables

\(\Delta v\)
ideal change in vehicle speed
\(I_{sp}\)
engine specific impulse
\(g_0\)
standard gravitational acceleration
\(m_0\)
mass before the burn, including upper stages and payload
\(m_f\)
mass after propellant is consumed, before staging

References and model inputs

  1. SpaceX, “Starship” — current V3 dimensions, propellant, engine counts, thrust, and payload.
  2. SpaceX EU Prospectus, 5 June 2026, p. 98 — 5,533 t Starship vehicle mass and 100+ t reusable LEO payload.
  3. SpaceX, “Falcon 9” and “Falcon Heavy” — current dimensions, total masses, engines, and payload capacities.
  4. NASA Historical Data Book, vol. II, table 1-67 — Saturn V stage dimensions, launch weights, and propulsion.
  5. Wikipedia, “SpaceX Starship”, “SpaceX Raptor”, “Falcon 9”, and “Saturn V” — rounded dry-mass and specific-impulse estimates where manufacturer values are unpublished.
